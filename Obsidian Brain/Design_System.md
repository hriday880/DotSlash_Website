# DotSlash Design System

## Theme & Aesthetic
The DotSlash website employs a "cyberpunk / hacker / minimal tech" aesthetic. This is characterized by:
- Dark mode primary surfaces (`void-black`, `surface-deep`).
- Glitch, float, pulse-glow, and blink animations.
- Heavy use of monospace fonts for a "coding" or "terminal" vibe.
- High contrast primary blue/indigo and cyan accents against stark white and silver text.

## Color Scheme

### Primary Accents
- **Primary (Blue/Indigo):** `#3300FF` (with lighter/darker shades from 50 to 900)
- **Cyan:** `#00D4FF` (with `#00AACC` for darker shade)

### Surfaces & Backgrounds
- **Void Black:** `#030303`
- **Surface Deep:** `#050505`
- **Surface:** `#131313`
- **Surface Containers:** `#1b1b1b` (Low), `#1f1f1f` (Normal), `#2a2a2a` (High), `#353535` (Highest / Variant)

### Text & Elements
- **Stark White:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Silver Accent:** `#A0A0A0`
- **On-Surface (Text):** `#e2e2e2`
- **On-Surface Variant (Muted):** `#c4c7c8`
- **Glimmer Gray:** `#1A1A1A`

## Typography

### Fonts
1. **Montserrat (sans-serif):** Used for primary, impactful headlines.
2. **Inter (sans-serif):** Used for standard body text and small uppercase labels.
3. **Space Mono / JetBrains Mono (monospace):** Used for a pixelated, developer-focused aesthetic on mono labels and code snippets.

### Scale
- **Headline Display:** 120px (Weight: 900, Line Height: 110px, tight tracking)
- **Headline Large:** 64px (Weight: 700, Line Height: 72px)
- **Headline Medium:** 32px (Weight: 600, Line Height: 40px)
- **Body Large:** 20px (Weight: 400, Line Height: 32px)
- **Body Medium:** 16px (Weight: 400, Line Height: 24px)
- **Label Mono:** 11px (Weight: 500, extra wide tracking for a terminal look)
- **Label Caps:** 10px (Weight: 700, extra wide tracking for uppercase subheadings)
