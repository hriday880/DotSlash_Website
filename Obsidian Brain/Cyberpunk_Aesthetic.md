# Cyberpunk Aesthetic

The core identity of DotSlash.

## Key Elements
- **Colors**: Butter Yellow (`#FFF8E7`) and Deep Blue (`#3300FF`). See [[Design_System]].
- **Typography**: Space Mono for that terminal feel.
- **Visuals**: No cheap CSS gradients. We use pure mathematics and `canvas` to render 3D ASCII waves.
- **Layout**: Glass-morphism, sharp borders, and raw tech interfaces.
